2026 Interns

Bergen Family Center's interns are an important part of our team, bringing energy, fresh ideas, and a passion for serving the community. Working alongside experienced professionals, they support programs for children, families, and older adults while gaining valuable hands-on experience in nonprofit and human services. Their contributions strengthen our ability to deliver high-quality programs and help ensure that more individuals and families receive the support they need to thrive.
